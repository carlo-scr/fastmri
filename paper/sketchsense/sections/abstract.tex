\begin{abstract}
Diffusion priors enable test-time active sensing without training an
acquisition policy, but existing methods commonly estimate uncertainty with
multiple posterior trajectories.  We study an ensemble-free alternative.
Around selected states of one measurement-guided trajectory, \method{} pushes
a small number of finite-difference denoiser Jacobian--vector products through
the complete linear candidate-measurement bank.  A protected Nystr\"om
factorization yields a positive-semidefinite, low-rank local
Tweedie-covariance surrogate in operator space.  The same factor supports
conditional local-Gaussian design: already acquired measurements initialize
the determinant state, and greedy log-determinant updates avoid selecting
multiple atoms that expose the same uncertainty direction.  We separate
mask quality from reconstruction effects by replaying every final mask with
one canonical sampler, and evaluate grouped SENSE lines and natural-image
blocks with actual denoiser-call accounting.  \textbf{Results are pending
corrected large-scale experiments; this working abstract intentionally makes
no empirical superiority claim.}
\end{abstract}
