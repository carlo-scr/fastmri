\section{Introduction}

An active sensing system chooses not only how to reconstruct an object, but
which measurement to buy next.  Diffusion priors are attractive in this
setting because a pretrained model can supply an image prior without a
task-specific acquisition network.  The difficulty is uncertainty.  A single
guided trajectory produces one reconstruction path, whereas acquisition
requires comparing many feasible future observations.

Recent diffusion active-sensing methods solve this problem with samples.
AdaSense repeatedly estimates projected posterior covariance from complete
posterior runs~\cite{elata2024adasense}; ADS maintains parallel particles
inside the reverse process~\cite{nolan2025ads}; diffusion-guided MRI Bayesian
experimental design estimates information gain by Monte
Carlo~\cite{iollo2025bedmri}.  These are strong baselines, but their cost grows
with the number of particles or trajectories.

We ask a narrower question: can a few local denoiser products reveal enough
\emph{correlation} between candidate measurements to design a useful batch?
For an ideal MMSE denoiser, Tweedie's identity connects its Jacobian with a
local denoising covariance~\cite{efron2011tweedie,manor2024posterior}.  Rather
than materialize that image-space matrix, \method{} applies the candidate bank
and its adjoint around a single measurement-guided state.  Randomized products
form a PSD rank-$q$ operator-space sketch.  A conditional D-optimal objective
then accounts for measurements already owned and updates after every atom in
the prospective batch.

This is not a claim of exact inverse-problem posterior covariance.  The
unconditional learned denoiser is evaluated at a state shaped by data
consistency; its Jacobian is a local surrogate that can be asymmetric or
indefinite.  We therefore expose PSD defects, calibrate against conditional
particles, and predeclare a no-submit gate if the correlations do not survive
both domains.

Our intended contributions are:
\begin{itemize}
  \item an ensemble-free mechanism that sketches the covariance surrogate of
  a general \emph{linear} candidate-measurement bank from a few JVPs of one
  live trajectory;
  \item observed-set-conditioned marginal and joint acquisition, including
  existing linear observations outside the candidate atomization;
  \item limited PSD, submodularity, and perturbation guarantees for the
  approximate local model, with explicit learned-Jacobian failure criteria;
  \item a compute-accounted protocol that separates system reconstructions
  from canonical final-mask replay across multi-coil MRI and held-out
  ImageNet-256.
\end{itemize}
