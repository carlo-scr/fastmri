\section{Properties of the Local Model}

\paragraph{PSD and rank.}
If $C_t\succeq0$, then the retained core eigenspace is positive and
$\widehat C_t=LL^\top\succeq0$ with
$\rank(\widehat C_t)\leq q$.  With an imperfect denoiser, this statement
applies to the protected surrogate, not necessarily to the raw asymmetric or
indefinite operator product.

\paragraph{Conditional greedy design.}
Under the PSD surrogate and independent Gaussian measurement noise, $F_t$ is
normalized, monotone, and submodular over grouped atoms.  The conditional
objective $F_t(M\cup S)-F_t(M)$ inherits these properties in $S$; hence
cardinality-constrained greedy selection achieves the standard
$1-1/e$ guarantee relative to the optimum of the \emph{same approximate local
model}~\cite{nemhauser1978analysis}.  This guarantee says nothing about model
mismatch to the true multimodal inverse-problem posterior.

\paragraph{Utility sensitivity.}
For a set containing $m$ scalar measurements, let $A$ and $\widehat A$ be the
true and sketched covariances whitened by measurement noise.  If both are PSD
and $\|A-\widehat A\|_2\leq\delta$, Weyl's inequality and the
$1$-Lipschitz denominator of $\log(1+x)$ on $x\geq0$ give
\begin{equation}
 \left|\tfrac12\log\det(I+A)
 -\tfrac12\log\det(I+\widehat A)\right|
 \leq \frac{m}{2}\log(1+\delta).
\end{equation}
We use this only to connect covariance calibration to design error; empirical
conditional-particle and final-utility calibration remains necessary.
