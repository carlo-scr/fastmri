\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:experiments}

\paragraph{Evidence policy.}
All pre-audit PVAS outputs are excluded.  Every reported run must record its
Git commit, input revision and hashes, per-sample seeds, final atom set,
measurement budget, actual denoiser evaluations, wall time, peak memory, and
per-image or per-volume metrics.  We report system reconstruction separately
from a canonical passive replay of each final mask.

\paragraph{Analytic falsification.}
Controlled real and complex Gaussian problems measure diagonal and covariance
error, subspace angles, log-determinant error, top-$k$ overlap, and selection
regret versus exact $C_t$.  Tests include non-idempotent covariance,
heteroscedastic noise, grouped complex atoms, nonsymmetric/indefinite
Jacobians, ill-conditioned operators, existing-set conditioning, and a
multimodal mixture.

\paragraph{Natural images.}
CIFAR-10 is development-only.  The headline experiment uses 250
deterministically selected images from the canonical 50,000-file ILSVRC2012
validation layout, disjoint from a reserved 16-image calibration prefix, and
OpenAI's unconditional ImageNet-256 ADM prior.  The
operator reveals grouped full-resolution blocks while a box-averaged image is
always observed.  Metrics are PSNR, SSIM, LPIPS, NFE, time, and memory.

\paragraph{Multi-coil MRI.}
We use fastMRI brain Cartesian SENSE lines at $R\in\{4,8\}$ and extra-line
budgets $L\in\{8,16\}$.  The target is at least 20 volume-disjoint validation
volumes using all slices, with inference and paired bootstrap intervals at volume level.
The predeclared primary MRI cell is $R=4,L=8$; the other three settings are
robustness and compute-frontier checks.
Primary metrics are RSS-target NMSE, PSNR, and SSIM; SENSE-relative values
are diagnostic.  We apply the fastMRI metric formulas to a declared fixed
center-$320$ crop, not the variable native-width challenge support, because
the current prior reconstructs $384\times320$ inputs.  Known simulated
complex-noise power or acquisition-valid calibration defines $\Gamma$.
Sampler scaling uses the noisy acquired-ACS RSS 99th percentile; a clean-ACS
statistic defines only the controlled synthetic noise amplitude.

\paragraph{Policies.}
Controls include exact-budget variable-density/equispaced masks, static and
adaptive random timing controls, corrected cross-Hutchinson marginal
selection, conditioned low-rank marginal selection, conditioned joint
\method{}, empirical covariance with marginal and log-det scoring, faithful
ADS and AdaSense implementations, diffusion MRI BED, Free Hunch,
Christoffel-DPS on a supported common operator, and a small exhaustive
canonical final-utility oracle.  Retrospective residual/error policies are
references, not upper bounds.

\paragraph{Primary test and go/no-go.}
The primary comparison is joint versus conditioned low-rank marginal
selection under canonical replay, with $R=4,L=8$ fixed as the MRI primary
cell.  We require a positive paired interval in both domains, with at least
$0.2$ dB in one, unless the weaker quality effect is offset by a decisive
compute/memory frontier.  Rank-$4$, one-round MRI
costs $T+2q=28$ versus $T=20$ passive NFEs.  Failure triggers repositioning or
no submission, not a diagonal-only novelty claim.
