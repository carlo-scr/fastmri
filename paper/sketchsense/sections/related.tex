\section{Related Work}

\paragraph{Diffusion active sensing.}
AdaSense established zero-shot projected-variance acquisition across
compressive imaging, MRI, and CT~\cite{elata2024adasense}.  ADS performs
mid-trajectory acquisition using particle entropy~\cite{nolan2025ads}.
Iollo et al.\ formulate diffusion-guided Bayesian experimental design for
MRI~\cite{iollo2025bedmri}, and Christoffel-DPS develops offline and online
sensor placement beyond Gaussian design assumptions~\cite{christoffel2026dps}.
Consequently, we do not claim the first training-free, adaptive, variance
based, or mid-trajectory diffusion sensing method.  Our empirical question is
whether an operator-pushed local JVP sketch offers a favorable
correlation-quality/compute tradeoff.

\paragraph{Diffusion covariance.}
Tweedie second moments and learned-denoiser Jacobians have been used for
uncertainty and inverse problems~\cite{manor2024posterior,peng2024covariance,
boys2024tmpd}.  Free Hunch obtains structured low-rank covariance information
from trajectory statistics with no extra denoiser
calls~\cite{rissanen2025freehunch}; Schioppa and Salimans study a one-JVP
structured covariance approximation~\cite{schioppa2026covaware}.  These works
rule out novelty claims for JVPs, low-rank covariance, or Tweedie moments
alone.  We compare their covariance constructions as acquisition inputs.

\paragraph{Experimental design and randomized linear algebra.}
Log-determinant Gaussian design and its submodularity are
classical~\cite{krause2005nearoptimal,nemhauser1978analysis}; randomized range
finding and Nystr\"om approximations are likewise
established~\cite{halko2011finding}.  \method{} combines these tools in the
candidate-measurement space of a live diffusion trajectory.  The contribution
must therefore be supported by the conditional mechanism, efficiency, and
cross-domain evidence rather than by presenting the ingredients as new.
