\section{Limitations and Ethics}

\method{} is local and Gaussian.  A single trajectory can commit to one mode,
so the sketch may miss uncertainty between distant posterior modes.
Finite-difference JVPs add denoiser calls and depend on step size.  A learned
Jacobian may be asymmetric or indefinite; PSD protection makes the design
well-defined but can change its meaning.  The method currently supports
linear candidate banks only, uses a different pretrained prior per image
domain, and has no guarantee that its local-model utility improves final
reconstruction.

Retrospective active-acquisition experiments reveal measurements from a
complete held-out scan only to simulate a prospective query.  Full data must
not leak into noise calibration, sensitivity estimation beyond acquired ACS,
or deployable scores.  MRI results are research evidence, not a clinical
claim.  ImageNet and fastMRI use must follow their respective licenses and
privacy terms.

At the time of this scaffold, large-scale corrected results, faithful external

must be resolved in the main paper; WACV Round 2 has no rebuttal in which to
