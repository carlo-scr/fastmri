\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Training-free diffusion solvers for inverse problems.}
A pretrained diffusion prior can solve linear inverse problems by interleaving
denoising with data-consistency updates: DPS \cite{chung2023dps} via the
likelihood gradient around the Tweedie estimate; \pigdm{} \cite{song2023pigdm}
via a Gaussian posterior approximation whose variance is a hand-crafted
\emph{scalar} shared across coordinates; DDRM \cite{kawar2022ddrm} and DDNM
\cite{wang2023ddnm} via consistency in the operator's spectral and null space.
Score-based priors reached compressed-sensing MRI early
\cite{jalal2021csgm,chung2022scoremri}. All of these solvers are
\emph{passive}: the measurement set is fixed before sampling begins.

\paragraph{Posterior covariance in diffusion models.}
A second line estimates the per-coordinate posterior variance
$\mathrm{diag}\,\mathrm{Cov}[\xz \mid \xt]$, which by Tweedie's second-moment
identity equals $\sigt^2$ times the diagonal of the denoiser Jacobian
\cite{manor2024posterior,efron2011tweedie}: for uncertainty quantification in
denoising \cite{manor2024posterior}; via autodiff vector--Jacobian row sums
(TMPD \cite{boys2024tmpd}); as optimal diagonal covariances for guidance
\cite{peng2024covariance}; via a temporal finite difference of scores (CA-DPS
\cite{hamidi2024cadps}); as a DCT-diagonal plus low-rank covariance tracked
from denoiser calls the sampler already makes (Free Hunch
\cite{rissanen2025freehunch}); via a Hutchinson probe with frequency-block
structure for unconditional sampling \cite{schioppa2026covaware}; and,
concurrently, in closed form for flow matching from the divergence of the
learned velocity field \cite{divergence2026}. We \emph{adopt} this estimator
family rather than extend it: our single-probe central-difference JVP is a
numerical variant of these estimators (Sec.~\ref{sec:method}), and the
Kalman-style consistency gain it induces is structurally anticipated by
\pigdm{} and, in substance, by
\cite{peng2024covariance,hamidi2024cadps,rissanen2025freehunch}. Our delta is what the map is
\emph{for}: every work above targets reconstruction (or unconditional
sampling) under a fixed operator; we repurpose the same statistic as a
mid-trajectory acquisition score.

\paragraph{Active acquisition.}
The trained-policy line learns what to measure from data---adversarial
evaluators \cite{zhang2019active}, learned probabilistic masks
\cite{bahadir2019loupe}, task-adaptive samplers
\cite{huijben2020dps,vangorp2021adps}, RL and greedy policies
\cite{pineda2020rl,bakker2020greedy}, end-to-end sampler--reconstructor
training \cite{yin2021seqmri}, and learned token uncertainty
\cite{ayzenberg2026token}. These require task-specific training, and a careful
multi-coil study found that learned ``adaptive'' policies can collapse to
effectively non-adaptive masks \cite{bakker2022multicoil}. The training-free
line replaces the policy with uncertainty derived from a generative posterior,
an idea predating diffusion-based systems \cite{vandecamp2023eig}: AdaSense
\cite{elata2024adasense} (projected posterior variance over repeated complete
DDRM sampling rounds), ADS \cite{nolan2025ads} (mid-trajectory entropy of a
bank of parallel DPS particles), Christoffel-DPS \cite{christoffel2026dps}
(online sensor placement from the Christoffel function of parallel DPS
chains; ensemble counts in Tab.~\ref{tab:positioning}), Diffusion Active
Learning \cite{barba2025dal} (CT angles by ensemble projected variance),
Iollo \etal~\cite{iollo2025bedmri} (multi-sample Monte-Carlo EIG for
k-space), and ensemble-based systems for spectrum cartography
\cite{gu2025gsc}, NMR \cite{goffinet2025diffnmr}, and ultrasound
\cite{vannierop2025cognitive}. Across all of these, acquisition-grade
uncertainty is purchased with an ensemble of sampling trajectories; no prior
method selects measurements from an ensemble-free, single-trajectory
posterior-variance estimate---the gap \pvas{} fills
(Tab.~\ref{tab:positioning}).
