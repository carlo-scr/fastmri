\section{Derivation of the frequency-resolved gain}
\label{app:derivation}

\authornote{Write out the following in full, in your own derivation.
(1) Stationary complex Gaussian prior $x_0\sim\mathcal{CN}(0,\bF^H\diag(S)\bF)$ with VE
forward process $x_t=x_0+\sigma_t\varepsilon$. Then
$\mathrm{Cov}[x_0\mid x_t]=\bF^H\diag(p(t))\bF$ with $p_i=S_i\sigma_t^2/(S_i+\sigma_t^2)$.
(2) Insert into \eqref{eq:kalman} with $\bA=\bM\bF$ to obtain \eqref{eq:gain}.
(3) The isotropic limit $S_i\to\infty$.
(4) The equivalence with the CS231N parametrisation $\hat\sigma_i^2=\sigma_y^2\sigma_t^2/p_i$.
(5) Multi-coil: Woodbury identity giving \eqref{eq:cg}, and positive definiteness, which
guarantees CG convergence.
(6) EDM channel convention: per-real-channel variance $\sigma_t^2$ means complex
variance $2\sigma_t^2$. Check against \texttt{src/samplers/multicoil.py}.}
